    How TravelLens works

    1. Search: location suggestions come from the Open-Meteo Geocoding API.
    2. Combine: the selected coordinates request live weather while your chosen currencies request a current conversion rate.
    3. Explain: the app presents the returned JSON data as an accessible, readable travel snapshot.

    Weather and exchange rates can change. TravelLens is a learning project and should not be the sole basis for financial or safety decisions.
